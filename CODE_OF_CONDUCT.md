# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning
  from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission
- Conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They
will take fair corrective action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct. They will explain moderation
decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents
the project in public. Representation includes using an official project account,
posting through an official social account, or acting as an appointed representative at
an online or offline event.

## Report a problem

Do not include incident details in a public issue.

Contact the repository owner through a private channel listed on
[@wilbertliu's GitHub profile](https://github.com/wilbertliu). If no private channel is
available, open a
[private contact request](https://github.com/wilbertliu/mailrovers/issues/new?template=private-contact.yml)
without incident details. A maintainer will arrange a private reporting channel.

Community leaders will review reports promptly and fairly. They must protect the privacy
and safety of anyone who reports an incident.

## Enforcement guidelines

Community leaders will use these guidelines when deciding the consequences of a
violation.

### 1. Correction

**Community impact:** Inappropriate language or other behavior considered unprofessional
or unwelcome.

**Consequence:** A private written warning that explains the violation and why the
behavior was inappropriate. Community leaders may request a public apology.

### 2. Warning

**Community impact:** A violation through one incident or a series of actions.

**Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior. The person must
avoid interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited contact with those
enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a stated period. Breaking these terms may lead to a
temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

**Community impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including sustained
inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from interaction or public communication with the
community. During this period, the person must not contact the people involved or those
enforcing the Code of Conduct. Breaking these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

**Community impact:** A pattern of violating community standards, sustained inappropriate
behavior, harassment, or aggression toward an individual or group.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from public interaction with the community.
